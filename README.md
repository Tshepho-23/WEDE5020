Kasi Kicks Website Project Proposal
Introduction

This proposal outlines the development of a modern and user-friendly website for Kasi Kicks, a Soweto-based sneaker and streetwear retail business. The purpose of the website is to establish a strong online presence for the business and provide customers with an easy way to view available sneakers, apparel, accessories, store information, and make product enquiries.

Background

Kasi Kicks was established in 2019 and has grown from a small sneaker-selling operation into a retail business serving customers in Soweto and Johannesburg. The business focuses on providing quality sneakers and streetwear at affordable prices while maintaining strong connections with the local community and youth culture. The proposed website will help represent this identity online and make information about the business more accessible to customers.

Objectives

The main objectives of the website are to:

Create a professional online presence for Kasi Kicks.
Display sneakers, apparel, and accessories in an organised catalogue.
Provide customers with product prices and available sizes.
Allow customers to make product and general enquiries online.
Provide accurate information about the business and its history.
Display the locations, operating hours, and contact details of the stores.
Create a responsive website that is accessible on both desktop and mobile devices.
Reflect the streetwear and kasi culture associated with the Kasi Kicks brand.
Target Audience

The primary target audience is teenagers and young adults between the ages of 16 and 30 in Johannesburg, particularly customers from Soweto and surrounding areas who are interested in sneakers, streetwear, and youth culture. The website is designed to appeal to customers who shop both online and in physical stores.

Proposed Website Features

The website will contain several main sections, including a Home page, About section, Products section, Enquiry section, and Contact section. The Products section will be divided into sneakers, apparel, and accessories, allowing users to easily find the products they are interested in. Customers will also be able to submit enquiries about stock availability, sizes, pricing, and bulk orders.

The Contact section will provide information about the Soweto flagship store and Braamfontein pop-up location, including addresses, opening hours, and telephone numbers. The website will also include navigation links that allow users to move easily between different sections.

Technologies

The website will be developed using HTML5 for the structure, CSS3 for the visual design and responsive layout, and JavaScript for interactive functionality such as navigation and enquiry forms. Images and custom graphics will also be used to strengthen the visual identity of the Kasi Kicks brand.

Expected Outcome

The completed website is expected to provide Kasi Kicks with a professional digital platform that improves communication with customers and makes its products and services easier to discover. It will strengthen the brand's online presence while providing customers with a convenient way to browse products, learn about the business, and make enquiries.

Conclusion

The Kasi Kicks website will combine functionality, accessibility, and a strong visual identity to create an online platform that represents the business and its connection to Soweto's streetwear culture. By providing clear product information, store details, and enquiry functionality, the website will help Kasi Kicks reach a wider audience and provide a better overall customer experience.
